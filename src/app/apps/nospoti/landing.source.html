Before you press play.

Do I need an account or AI API key?

You can listen as a guest. An account is used for supported cloud library sync. Normal music listening does not require an AI API key.

Will every imported song play?

Playlist imports bring song information into your library. Playback needs an available matching source, so some tracks may be unavailable.

Can I listen without internet?

Only audio already available on your device can play offline. Online searches, new streams, account sync, and Jam rooms need internet.

Are these app screenshots?

The large hero and mobile images are design mockups from the original NoSpoti landing page. The current app may look different.

How is my data stored?

Guest library data is stored locally. Signed-in accounts use Supabase for supported account and library data. Account and library sync uses network requests to Supabase; local file storage depends on your browser.
